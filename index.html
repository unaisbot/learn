About the event

Explore the science behind species distributions

Ecological Niche Modelling (ENM) is an important approach in ecology and biodiversity research. It uses species occurrence records and environmental variables to identify areas with suitable environmental conditions for a species. These models support the study of species distributions, habitat suitability, potential range shifts and ecological responses to environmental change.

This five-day hybrid certificate course introduces participants to the concepts, datasets and analytical workflow involved in ecological niche modelling using MaxEnt. The programme is designed to connect ecological understanding with geospatial data processing and model interpretation.

Course syllabus

Topics covered during the course

A structured outline of the major themes. Detailed subtopics can be expanded as the final syllabus is approved.

01

Introduction to Ecological Niche Modelling

Ecological niche concepts; species distribution modelling; theoretical foundations; applications in ecology, biogeography and conservation.

02

Species Occurrence Data

Occurrence data sources; data formats; coordinate validation; duplicate records; spatial filtering and data preparation.

03

Environmental Data and Predictor Selection

Bioclimatic variables; environmental raster layers; variable selection; multicollinearity; resolution and extent.

04

GIS-Based Data Preparation

Spatial reference systems; raster processing; clipping and masking; raster alignment; preparation of input files.

05

MaxEnt Model Development

MaxEnt software; model inputs and settings; feature classes; regularisation; model execution and output generation.

06

Model Evaluation and Interpretation

Model performance; evaluation metrics; response curves; variable contributions; suitability classes and map interpretation.

07

Applications in Ecological Research

Habitat suitability assessment; invasive species research; climate change applications; conservation planning and decision support.

08

Practical Workflow and Discussion

Integrated modelling workflow; common challenges; interpretation of outputs; research applications and participant discussion.
