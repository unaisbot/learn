About the Course

Ecological Niche Modelling (ENM) is an important approach in ecology and biodiversity research. It uses species occurrence records and environmental variables to identify areas with suitable environmental conditions for a species. These models provide valuable insights into species distributions, habitat suitability, potential range shifts and ecological responses to environmental change. ENM has wide-ranging applications in biodiversity conservation, invasive species management, endangered species protection, habitat restoration, climate change impact assessment, protected area planning and the identification of potential invasion hotspots. It also supports evidence-based conservation strategies and informed ecological decision-making.

The Tropical Biodiversity Action and Research Centre (TBARC), in association with the Postgraduate and Research Department of Botany, Korambayil Ahamed Haji Memorial Unity Women's College (Autonomous), Manjeri, organises a Five-Day Hybrid Certificate Course on Ecological Niche Modelling Using MaxEnt.

This five-day hybrid certificate course introduces participants to the fundamental concepts, essential datasets and analytical workflows involved in ecological niche modelling using MaxEnt. The programme is designed to bridge ecological understanding with geospatial data processing, species occurrence data preparation, environmental variable selection, model development, validation and interpretation. Through a combination of expert-led sessions and practical training, participants will gain an understanding of how ENM can be applied to real-world ecological and biodiversity research questions.

Course syllabus

Detailed Course Syllabus

A five-day learning programme combining theoretical sessions and practical training in ecological niche modelling.

Course Objective

Provide participants with theoretical knowledge and hands-on training in Ecological Niche Modeling (ENM) using MaxEnt, QGIS and R, enabling them to independently develop, evaluate and interpret species distribution models for conservation, ecology and climate change applications.

Day01
December 87:30 pm – 9:30 pm2 hoursOnline

Fundamentals of Ecological Niche Modeling

Topics Covered

  • Introduction to Ecological Niche Modeling (ENM)
  • Ecological niche concepts and Species Distribution Models (SDMs)
  • Overview of modelling approaches (presence-only, presence–absence, machine learning)
  • Introduction to MaxEnt: principles, assumptions, advantages and disadvantages
  • Applications of ENM in biodiversity conservation, invasive species, climate change and restoration
  • Limitations of ENM and situations where ENM should not be applied
  • Discussion of published case studies and complete ENM workflow

Learning Outcomes

  • Understand the concepts and workflow of ENM.
  • Differentiate modelling approaches and identify appropriate applications.
  • Recognize strengths and limitations of MaxEnt.

December 97:30 pm – 9:30 pm2 hoursOnline

Software Installation and Data Acquisition

  • Basic processing and management of spatial datasets

Learning Outcomes

  • Install and configure all required software.
  • Acquire and organize occurrence and environmental datasets.
  • Understand raster and vector data structures.
